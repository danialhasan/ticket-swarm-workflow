# Ledger Categories

Every row in the feature decision ledger must land in exactly one of these buckets.

## Current beta thesis

Use for:

- the one sentence describing what the beta must prove
- the bounded user relief the team is protecting

There should be one current thesis at a time.

## Explicit beta non-goal

Use for:

- feature areas that are intentionally out of scope for the current beta
- canon-cut categories that the team wants visible during planning

## Parking-lot feature

Use for:

- valid future feature families that should be preserved without expanding the beta
- ideas that already map to a holding epic or clearly deserve one

## Kill-list candidate

Use for:

- feature ideas or work items that actively threaten beta focus
- scope that should be removed, declined, or held out of the launch

Kill-list rows should state why the idea is dangerous now, not merely why it is "not my favorite."

## Discussed but undecided

Use for:

- ideas that came up repeatedly but still lack an explicit yes or no
- unresolved questions that affect roadmap or launch planning

Prefer this bucket over silent ambiguity.
